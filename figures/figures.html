In plain language
Chunk - a short passage of a standard.
Embedding - a numeric fingerprint of meaning.
Vector index - finds meaning, not exact words.
Keyword index - catches exact codes like D5199.
Reranking - puts the best passage first.
Grounded - written only from what was found.
